Lore

The league doesn't recruit — it rolls. Every pilot who enters the grid gets a call sign, a tier, and a starting build, generated the moment they're minted. Nobody chooses their rarity. Nobody chooses their name.

Common pilots make up the bulk of the field: fast to mint, quick to race, built for volume. Mythic pilots are rumor until you see one on the line — one in a hundred, running stats the rest of the grid can't touch.

Season 01 opens the grid to 10,000 pilots. What happens after that is decided by the people holding them.

Stats & gameplay

Three core stats, rolled once at mint:

  • Speed — top-line velocity on the straights.
  • Armor — how much contact a pilot can take across a race.
  • Boost — burst acceleration for overtakes and closing sprints.

These stats aren't cosmetic. They load straight into your in-game build when the closed race season opens — no grinding, no separate setup.

Minting guide

FAQ

How is rarity decided?

Each pilot rolls one of five tiers at mint, weighted 60/25/10/4/1 for Common through Mythic. The roll is final and can't be changed after mint.

Do stats matter after mint?

Yes. Speed, armor, and boost are set at mint based on tier and carry into the racing game once the closed season opens.
