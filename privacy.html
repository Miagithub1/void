What we collect

The whitelist form on this site collects the email address, X (Twitter) username, and EVM wallet address you submit. This data is stored in a Google Sheet used only to manage early access to mint.

What we don't collect

This site does not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising pixels. Wallet connections (once live) only read your public wallet address — never your private keys or funds.

How your data is used

Whitelist submissions are used solely to confirm early mint access and to contact you about it, typically via the X handle or wallet address provided. Data is not sold or shared with third parties.

On-chain data

Once minting is live, your wallet address and transaction history for this collection become public on the blockchain, as with any NFT mint — this is inherent to how blockchains work, not something this site controls.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests to remove submitted whitelist data, can be raised in the project's Discord.
